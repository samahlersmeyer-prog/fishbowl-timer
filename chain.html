Chain Reaction

⏱ Fishbowl 🦃 Jeopardy

Each word goes with the one above it and the one below it (FIRE → TRUCK → STOP → SIGN). The team that's up picks a hidden word next to a solved one and guesses it. Get it right and you keep going. Get it wrong and you get another letter, and the next team can steal it. Each word is worth 10 points for every letter still hidden, and the team that finishes the chain gets a 50-point bonus.
